Delete your NEXRYDE account

You can delete your account from the NEXRYDE mobile app while signed in, or request deletion by email if you cannot access the app.

Delete in the app (recommended)
  1. Open the NEXRYDE app and sign in.
  2. Riders: go to Settings and scroll to Delete account.
  3. Drivers: open Profile and use Delete account.
  4. Confirm when prompted. Your account will be deactivated and scheduled for deletion.
Cannot sign in?

Email support@nexryde.com with the subject line Account deletion request. Include the email address or phone number you used to register so we can verify ownership.

For privacy-specific requests, you may also contact privacy@nexryde.com.

What happens when you delete

Your account is marked as deleted and deactivated. Associated profile and driver status are updated so you no longer appear as an active user. Push notification tokens are removed. We may retain certain records where required by law (for example, transaction or safety-related data) for a limited period.
