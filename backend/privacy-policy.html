Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 3, 2026  |  Version 2.0  |  Complies with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA)

NEXRYDE (a product of AdmoblordGroup) ("we", "us", or "NEXRYDE") operates the NEXRYDE mobile application and related services. We are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, what data we collect, why we collect it, how we use and share it, how long we keep it, and what rights you have under Nigerian law.

Quick summary: We collect only what is necessary to run a safe, reliable ride-sharing service. We do not sell your personal data. We never share your data with advertisers. You can request your data or ask us to delete your account at any time from within the app.

2. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, we process your personal data on the following legal grounds:

  • Contract performance: To provide the ride-sharing service you have requested.
  • Legal obligation: To comply with Nigerian laws including tax, anti-fraud, and safety regulations.
  • Legitimate interests: To improve our services, prevent fraud, and maintain platform security.
  • Consent: For optional features such as trip safety recordings and face unlock login — you can withdraw consent at any time from app Settings.

3. How We Share Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share it only in these specific cases:

  • Between riders and drivers during a trip: First name, rating, vehicle details (make, model, plate), and real-time GPS. This sharing ends when the trip is complete.
  • Emergency contacts: Location and trip details shared only when you activate the SOS or Trip Sharing feature.
  • Service providers: Google Maps (routing), Termii (SMS OTP), Squad (payment processing). Each is bound by its own data protection agreements and our data processing contracts.
  • Legal requirement: Where required by valid Nigerian court order, law enforcement request, or regulatory obligation.
  • Business transfer: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of NEXRYDE, your data may be transferred to the successor entity subject to the same privacy protections.

4. Data Security

  • All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS 1.2+.
  • API access is protected by JWT-based authentication with short-lived tokens.
  • Sensitive data (tokens, device IDs) is stored using platform-level secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).
  • Access to production databases is restricted to authorised NEXRYDE personnel with audit logging.
  • Face images used for verification are not stored on-device; they are processed securely on our servers and retained only as a hash fingerprint.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we implement industry-standard safeguards, we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a data breach that is likely to result in significant harm to you, we will notify you and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) within 72 hours as required by the NDPA 2023.

6. Your Rights Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023

Access Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
Correction Ask us to correct any inaccurate or incomplete data.
Deletion Request deletion of your account and personal data (subject to legal retention obligations).
Portability Receive your data in a machine-readable format.
Objection Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
Restrict Processing Ask us to pause processing while a dispute is resolved.
Withdraw Consent Turn off consent-based features (face unlock, safety recording) from in-app Settings.
Complain Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) at ndpc.gov.ng.

To exercise any of the above rights, email privacy@nexryde.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Location Data

Location access is essential to the ride-sharing service. The app requests foreground location during booking and in-progress rides. Background location is requested only when a driver is online and waiting for trip offers. You can adjust location permissions in your device settings, but disabling location will prevent you from booking or receiving rides.

8. Cookies and Analytics

The NEXRYDE mobile app does not use browser cookies. We use anonymised, aggregated in-app analytics to understand feature usage and improve the product. No personally identifiable information is included in analytics data.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed on servers hosted by Google Cloud (us-central1 region) and MongoDB Atlas. These providers are bound by contractual data protection obligations and comply with international data protection standards. Where data is transferred outside Nigeria, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

10. Children's Privacy

NEXRYDE is not intended for persons under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we discover that a minor has created an account, we will delete the account and all associated data immediately. If you believe a minor is using NEXRYDE, please notify us at support@nexryde.com.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. For material changes, we will notify you via in-app notification or email at least 14 days before the new policy takes effect, with the version number and effective date clearly displayed. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance.
