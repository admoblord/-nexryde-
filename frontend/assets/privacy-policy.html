NEXRYDE Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 14, 2026

NEXRYDE ("we", "us", or "our") operates the NEXRYDE mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our ride-sharing platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, phone number, email address, National Identification Number (NIN), profile photo.
  • Location Data: Real-time GPS location during trip booking, in-progress rides, and when drivers are online.
  • Trip Data: Pickup and dropoff locations, route, fare, distance, duration, and ratings.
  • Payment Information: Payment method preferences and transaction records. We do not store card numbers.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and app version for support and analytics.
  • Camera & Microphone: Used only for document verification, face verification, and voice booking when explicitly activated by you.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Match riders with nearby drivers and calculate fares.
  • Process payments and provide trip receipts.
  • Verify driver identity, vehicle documents, and NIN.
  • Enable safety features: SOS alerts, trip sharing, security codes, and route monitoring.
  • Improve our services through anonymized analytics.
  • Communicate important updates about your account or trips.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only in these cases:

  • Between Riders and Drivers: Name, rating, vehicle details, and real-time location during a trip.
  • Emergency Contacts: Location and trip data shared when you use the SOS feature or trip sharing.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by Nigerian law or to protect the safety of users.
  • Service Providers: Google Maps for routing, SMS providers for OTP verification — bound by their own privacy policies.

4. Data Security

We use encryption (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit, JWT-based authentication for API access, and secure storage for sensitive data. Access to user data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

5. Data Retention

We retain trip data for 2 years for dispute resolution and regulatory compliance. Account data is kept while your account is active and deleted within 90 days of account closure upon request.

6. Your Rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access and download your personal data.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your account and data.
  • Withdraw consent for optional data processing.

7. Location Permissions

Location access is essential for our ride-sharing service. You can disable background location access in your device settings, but the app requires foreground location to book and track rides.

8. Children's Privacy

NEXRYDE is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the app or via email.
