## 💰 **INTRA-CITY PRICING** (Within City, Max 50km)

### **Formula:**
```
Price = (Base Fare + (Distance × Rate per km)) × Vehicle Multiplier

Base Fare: ₦200
Rate per km: ₦100/km
```

### **Why This Works:**
- Short trips = Low price
- Long trips = Higher price
- Automatic calculation by map
- Transparent for riders
- Drivers keep 100% of fare

## 🛣️ **INTER-CITY PRICING** (City to City, 50km+)

### **Formula:**
```
Price = (Base Fare + (Distance × Rate per km) + (Hours × Rate per hour)) × Vehicle Multiplier

Base Fare: ₦1,000
Rate per km: ₦120/km
Rate per hour: ₦800/hour
```

### **Why Distance + Time?**

**Distance alone is NOT fair to drivers!**

- Lagos → Abuja: 750km, 9 hours
- If only distance: Driver earns ₦90,000 but loses 9 HOURS
- With time: Driver earns ₦105,200 (compensated for time!)

**Benefits:**
1. ✅ Driver is compensated for TIME spent driving
2. ✅ Fair payment for long trips
3. ✅ Drivers happy to accept inter-city rides
4. ✅ Riders still get cheaper prices than competitors
5. ✅ Transparent (riders see distance + time)

### **Example: Lagos → Ibadan (130km, 2 hours)**

|---------|-------------|-------|
| **Economy** | (₦1,000 + 130×₦120 + 2×₦800) × 1.0 | **₦18,200** |
